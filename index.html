An Engineered Bioactive Hydrogel
for Enhanced Angiogenesis

Healing factors from your own immune cells — delivered directly to the wound.

ANGel delivers special healing factors derived from neutrophils — a type of immune cell — into the wound bed through a hydrogel carrier. These factors shift the wound from a chronic inflammatory state to a pro-repair state by activating the body's macrophages.

The result: organized skin formation, decreased scarring, and a wound site rich with new blood vessels — the critical prerequisite for successful skin grafting.

  • Dual-barrel syringe delivery — fits tunneling or large wounds

Annual economic burden of chronic wounds in the United States.

Chronic wounds are a major clinical challenge for patients with diabetes, vascular disease, and old age — conditions where even small lesions can fail to heal properly. Wounds remain stuck in a prolonged state of inflammation and struggle to reach the proliferation stage where new blood vessels grow and skin cells migrate into the wound bed.

Wound healing and skin graft success ultimately depend on effective vascularization of the wound bed. Angiogenesis is promoted by anti-inflammatory M2 macrophage signaling. Chronic inflammation prevents pro-inflammatory M1 macrophages from differentiating into their pro-regenerative M2 state. When angiogenesis is prolonged or inhibited, infection wins.
